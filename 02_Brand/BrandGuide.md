# NextCreator Brand Guide

Version: 1.0

---

# Brand Name

NextCreator

---

# Tagline

Stop managing AI tools.
Start building your creator business.

---

# Elevator Pitch

NextCreator is an AI-powered creator workspace that transforms one content idea into a complete publishing roadmap.

Instead of switching between multiple AI tools, creators get one intelligent workspace that guides them from idea to strategy, storytelling, scripts, prompts, publishing, and consistency.

---

# Mission

Help aspiring creators become consistent creators through one intelligent workspace.

---

# Vision

Creators don't fail because they lack creativity.

They fail because they lack a system.

NextCreator provides that system.

# Primary Logo

Wordmark

NextCreator

The X is highlighted in purple and subtly represents forward movement, progress, and the creator's next step.

---

# Logo Usage

Primary

Dark text with purple X.

Monochrome

All black.

Dark Mode

White text with purple X.

Minimum clear space

Height of the letter N.

Never:

- stretch
- rotate
- add shadows
- use gradients
- change proportions
