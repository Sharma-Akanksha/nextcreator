## Vision

NextCreator is an AI-powered creator workspace that transforms one content idea into a complete publishing system.

Instead of switching between multiple AI tools, creators get one intelligent workspace that guides them from idea to research, storytelling, scripts, AI prompts, captions, publishing plans, and daily execution.

NextCreator doesn't just generate content.

It removes decision fatigue and helps creators publish consistently.

## Product Philosophy

### Goal → Roadmap

Every creator journey starts with a clear goal.

NextCreator turns that goal into a personalized Creator Roadmap — a step-by-step path from where they are to where they want to be.

The roadmap drives daily execution through Today's Focus, so creators always know what to work on next.

Goal → Roadmap → Today's Focus → Publish.

### Feature Filter

Every feature must answer one question:

"Does this help the creator publish consistently?"

If not, it doesn't belong in the MVP.
